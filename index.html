About PL Infralink

Building and powering India’s infrastructure

PL Infralink carries out construction, installation, maintenance and repair of civil, electrical and telecom infrastructure all over the country, with precision and practical solutions.

The company is built on the knowledge, ideas and experience of experts from each field. We are also growing in energy storage, with battery energy storage systems (BESS), utility-scale integration and next-generation power backup.

What kind of projects does PL Infralink take up?

Civil construction, electrical contracting, telecom infrastructure, electrical and automation panels, instrumentation and commissioning, battery energy storage, commercial EV charging, and architecture and interior design.

Do you work outside Odisha?

Yes. We carry out civil, electrical and telecom infrastructure work all over the country.

Do you manufacture electrical panels?

Yes. We manufacture panel boards, smart boards, and electrical and automation panels at our 20,000 sq ft unit in Bhubaneswar, and offer turnkey supply, installation and commissioning.
